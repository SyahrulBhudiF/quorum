Quorum is a free incremental game about system design. You are the first engineer of a startup, and you wire
Components on a board while requests flow through your Design. Each fix moves the bottleneck
somewhere else.

New to system design? [Learn system design by playing](/tutorials/learn-system-design) gives you an order, and
[System design concepts](/explanation/system-design-concepts) maps every idea to the problem it solves. Preparing for an interview? Start with the
[system design interview questions](/guides/system-design-interview-questions).
