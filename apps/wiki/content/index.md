Quorum is a free incremental game about system design. You are the first engineer of a startup, and you wire
Components on a board while requests flow through your Design. Each fix moves the bottleneck
somewhere else.

The name is not a mix-up with the distributed systems term. A **quorum** is the minimum number of replicas that must agree before a read or a write counts, as in [Quorum reads and writes](/patterns/quorum-reads-and-writes) and [Consensus and leader election](/patterns/consensus-and-leader-election). The game borrows the word for its name. When this wiki says Quorum with a capital Q, it means the game. A lowercase quorum is the technique.

[System design concepts](/explanation/system-design-concepts) maps every idea to the problem it solves. Preparing for an interview? Start with the
