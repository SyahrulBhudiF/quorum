at 34–41 sim minutes at the design pace, not 45–60 (see the divergence log). The vertical slice gate

that need later Components are not. The M4 playtest has not been run.

**M5 status.** Batches B0–B4 are in the build: the per-Component rule table, the Seed Era with
`search`, `upload`, and `bot`, the Search Engine, Object Storage, Queue, Worker, CDN, and WAF, egress,
Cache stampedes, Seed incidents, four Seed Features, and the Seed Sandbox track. Batches B5–B9 add Go,
Java, C#, Elixir, and Rust; the Series A Era with `media`, `rt`, and `buy`, the Payment Provider,
zones and standbys, the SLO and error budget, the Era-aware incident director, the Autoscaler and the
Kubernetes Cluster. Batches B10–B14 add the Series B Era with `job`, `report`, and `ai`, the Data
Warehouse, the GPU Node and the LLM API; Java collectors and GOGC; backoff, circuit breakers, load
shedding and idempotency keys; read-your-writes routing; Axum and Actix; Disk full and eight Series B
and Series A incidents; five Series B Features; the Stale Checkout Sandbox and the Series B Sandbox
track. From Series A, incidents run on 11's calendar durations, so the
SLO can be met (see the divergence log). Not built: Queue options (SQS, max length), GPU batch
size, quantization and model size, completion-time Apdex for async kinds, and the Margin bot. The Balanced bot raises Series A at 62.7–71.3 sim minutes and Series B at
112.2–121.3 on seeds 1–5, holding 97.6–99.0% availability; it has not shipped the Series B Features by
then.

**M6 status.** Batches B15–B22 are in the build: regions as tabs over one Design
([ADR 0005](../adr/0005-regions-are-tabs-over-one-design.md)) with regional demand and peaks, cross-region
latency and egress, and GeoDNS; the Series C, Pre-IPO, and Public Eras; `feed`, `hook`, and `push` with
the Push Provider and push or hybrid fan-out; hot keys and key splitting; the Wide-column Store with

endless Public quarters with guidance; 22 research items, six incidents, eight Features, and the Series

**M9 status.** Mute and Reduce motion settings (prefs record), incident, Era, and achievement sounds;

**M8 status.** Challenge mode, server verification, histograms, the daily drill, and the

**M7 status.** Exits, Legacy, the Founder record, the Legacy shop, Playbooks, Ventures, Mutators, and

## Scope rules

- The simulation stays a pure module with no UI imports, so it can run in tests, in the offline
  simulation, in Challenge verification on the server, and in the Tradeoff card preview.
- Sandboxes run only in the browser. They need no server, no account, and no histogram, so they ship
  with the milestone that adds their mechanics, not with Challenges in M8.
- The browser owns the company simulation ([ADR 0002](../adr/0002-endless-incremental-game-in-the-browser.md)).
  The API Worker owns only what needs a server: Challenge verification, histograms, and the daily
  drill. Those contracts go in `packages/rpc` and their handlers in `apps/backend`, as the repository
  guide requires.
- Content is data. A new Component, runtime, kind, incident, or research item is a catalog entry plus,
  at most, one new rule in the solver.

**Vertical slice gate.** The catalog is large. No content for M5 or later is written until M2 is
playable and a playtest shows that players meet a new bottleneck every few minutes
([pillar 3](./01-vision.md#pillar-3-the-bottleneck-is-the-content)). If the slice fails that test,
fix the loop before adding Components.

## Out of scope

- Multiplayer and shared companies.
- Accounts beyond what Challenge histograms need.
- Paid currency of any kind.
- A simulated Kubernetes cluster ([ADR 0002](../adr/0002-endless-incremental-game-in-the-browser.md)).
- Mobile layouts below 1024 px wide. The board needs space.

## Open questions

| Question                                                          | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Does the Team system make the idle part too busy?                 | M4 ships the fallback: the autopilot (on by default) queues the next Feature, refactors at 30% debt, and shifts built migration steps, so a player who ignores the Team still progresses. Whether the Team is too busy still needs a playtest.                                                                           |
| Is 100 ms the right step for 200 Nodes with the latency coupling? | Measured in M1 (`solve.bench.ts`, Node.js 26): one solve of a 200-Node board takes about 24 ms and the Bottleneck search about 24 ms, against budgets of 2 ms and 3 ms. Today's boards have 5 to 20 Nodes (the Pre-seed start solves in 0.23 ms), so the 100 ms Step stays. The solver needs about 12× before M5 boards. |
| Should regions be separate boards or one large board?             | Decided in [ADR 0005](../adr/0005-regions-are-tabs-over-one-design.md): one Design, one tab per region; a tab shows its region's Nodes and the remote Nodes they call.                                                                                                                                                   |
| How visible should runtime stereotypes be in copy?                | Copy names behaviors ("holds a worker while waiting"), never judgments ("slow language").                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Do Challenge histograms need an account?                          | Prefer an anonymous device ID with optional sign-in.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| How do we keep real outage references respectful?                 | Name the event and the year, link the public postmortem, and never mock the company.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Does the free Sandbox spoil discovery?                            | It unlocks everything from the start. Fallback: unlock each Component in the free Sandbox when the player meets it in a Company or a guided Sandbox.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Does the Dashboard pull players off the Board?                    | The Dashboard shows only history ([Observability](./12-observability.md#the-dashboard)). Playtest: count how often players find the bottleneck from the Dashboard instead of the Board. If it is more than one time in five, remove the per-Node Charts from the Overview row.                                           |
| Should the Public Era have an end?                                | Current answer: no end, but the Step down Exit gives a natural stopping point with a score.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
