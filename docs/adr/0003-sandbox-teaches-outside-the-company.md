# Sandboxes teach mechanics outside the Company

A Company teaches each mechanic when the player first needs it. The Garage Era is the tutorial, with
no tutorial screens. This works for the first 20 minutes. It does not work for later mechanics: to
learn zones or GC pauses, a player must first play a Company to Series A, which takes 2 to 6 hours,
and must do it again after each Exit.

Quorum adds the Sandbox, a Board outside any Company. A guided Sandbox teaches one mechanic from a
fixed Design through ordered objectives; its length varies with the mechanic, like a Factorio
tutorial level. The guided Sandboxes for each Era form a Sandbox track, available from
the start. Challenges follow a separate sequence across Eras. The free Sandbox is a test bench with
everything unlocked and unlimited Cash.

Considered options:

- Tutorial screens or prompts inside the Company. Rejected: a confusing tutorial is the top
  complaint about Upload Labs, and the game teaches through consequences.
- Challenges as the only mode outside the Company, unlocked by Era reached. Rejected: Challenges are
  for mastery, with scoring, histograms, and server verification, and Era gating keeps the replay
  problem.
- A Company that starts at a later Era. Rejected: it skips the Eras that make the late game make sense
  and weakens Legacy as the reason to found the next Company.
- Sandbox rewards in a Company, such as Cash, research, or Legacy. Rejected: a reward makes the
  Sandbox a required grind.

## Consequences

- The Garage Era stays the tutorial for a new player. A guided Sandbox has no text overlays or
  step-by-step prompts.
- Sandboxes run only in the browser and need no server, so each Sandbox track ships with the
  milestone that adds its mechanics.
- Each guided Sandbox is also a balance fixture: it must pass with its fix and fail with its wrong
  option, and the dominance test runs on it.
- Achievements do not count in any Sandbox.
